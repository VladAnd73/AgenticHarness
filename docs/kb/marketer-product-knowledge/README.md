## What this is
The **Marketer platform product-knowledge base** — written primarily for **AI agents** working across the Marketer repos, and useful to humans too. It is the *"what & why"* layer between technical / architecture docs and how-to runbooks: what the product does, for whom, in what vocabulary, and via what flows.
## Audience & perspective
- **Platform operators** — marketers, brokers, agencies, property developers: their goals, workflows, and vocabulary.
- **The paying customer organization** — company accounts, seats, roles, plans, and what a customer gets.
- **Internal Marketer staff (CSMs / ops)** — the internal back-office perspective: the staff-only surfaces (Admin Panel) used to configure, triage, and support customer accounts. *(Documented audience per the operator's scope ruling 2026-08-31; matches the Product & App Map Admin Panel row's Who — “Marketer staff (CSMs / ops), not customer users”.)*
- *Not covered:* end consumers / ad recipients (the people campaigns target).
## Focus & scope
<callout icon="🎯" color="blue_bg">
	**Deep focus:** M360 (incl. Broker Experience), New Builds, and the **M2 backend** — where most business logic, domain objects, and states live.
	**Supporting services — purpose, not internals:** targeter, publisher, CRM gateway. Document their purpose in the system, how users interact with them, and how they influence states / objects in M2 / M360 and the business — **not** their internal mechanics.
</callout>
## How this is maintained (trust rules)
- Every section page carries a **status**, a **last-verified date**, and a **sources** list.
- Every non-obvious claim is **cited** to its source (Confluence / Jira, Notion, a code path, or an app screen) so a reader can re-verify. **Uncited = treat as unverified.**
- Every section ends with a **"Gaps & uncertainties"** block.
